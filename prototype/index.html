What goes in

Four ingredients. All good ones.

  • Organic unbleached flourNo bleach, no bromate. Just wheat.
  • Filtered reverse osmosis waterClean water makes a cleaner rise.
  • Two sea saltsRedmond unrefined mineral salt in the dough, Maldon flakes on top.
  • Premium cold-pressed extra virgin olive oilGenerous on the pan and on the crust.

Plus a sourdough starter we've kept alive for a long time, and for the garlic pan, roasted garlic and fresh rosemary. No yeast packets, no preservatives.

About Ruby

"I started because people kept asking for more."

Ruby is 10. She pitches for her softball team and has been making sourdough focaccia with her mom since she was tall enough to reach the counter. Every time it came out at a get-together, someone asked when they could get their own pan.

So she's selling it. Ruby takes the orders, talks to customers, and keeps the money she earns. Her parents work for her.

RubyOwner. Orders, customers, quality control, and the money.
MomHead of oven. Dough, timing, and making sure nothing burns.
