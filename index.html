    About Ruby

    Rise and shine, baby.

    Ruby is 10. She loves softball, flag football, basketball, and riding horses. And she has always loved her mom's focaccia.

    So she decided to learn how to make it, and to start a business of her own. This is it: Ruby's Rise & Shine. She helps with every single part of it.

    Running it has taught Ruby to be responsible, to save her money, and how a real business works. What is she saving for? She's not sure yet. Maybe a horse.

    Most of all, she wants to share her love of focaccia with you.

    RubyOwner. Orders, customers, quality control, and the money.
    MomHead of oven. Dough, timing, and making sure nothing burns.
    DadWebsite guy. This page, the QR code, and deliveries when asked nicely.
